# Design explanation (for internship submission)

TaskFlow AI is presented as a calm, modern productivity SaaS product with a lavender accent palette, generous whitespace, and clear typography.
The hero dashboard preview gives visitors an immediate idea of how the product helps organize a workday.
The features and three-step workflow explain the product's value without overwhelming the visitor.
The pricing area includes Starter and Pro plans, with a working monthly/yearly price toggle.
The FAQ accordion and responsive mobile navigation make the page easier to explore on smaller screens.
The demo request form validates required fields and email format, then displays an on-page success confirmation.
The project is frontend-only, so form submissions are demonstrated locally and are not sent to a backend.
